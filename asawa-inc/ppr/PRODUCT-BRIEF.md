# PPR — Product Brief

## One-Liner

A personal wedding command center — tasks, research, and creative tools for a groom managing his July 5-6, 2026 wedding.

## The Bet

This works IF one centralized place for wedding tasks + research + creative tools prevents a groom from drowning in tabs, WhatsApp groups, and forgotten to-dos with 93 days until the wedding.

## User Persona

Abhishek, groom getting married July 5-6, 2026. Juggling shopping (T-shirts, suits, gifts), family duties, logistics, romance, and bride-related tasks across browser tabs, WhatsApp groups, and mental notes. Needs to coordinate with family, the bride, and vendors — without anything falling through the cracks.

## Current Alternative

Browser tabs, WhatsApp groups, mental notes, scattered lists. Nothing is centralized. Things get forgotten.

## Why That's Not Good Enough

- No single view of all wedding tasks across categories
- Research (fashion, shopping, comparisons) lives in random tabs that get lost
- Creative work (greeting cards, catalogs) requires separate tools (Canva, etc.)
- Can't share a clean summary with the bride or family via WhatsApp
- Every existing wedding tool is a vendor marketplace, not a personal planner

## Market Gap

Every major wedding platform (Zola, The Knot, Joy, WedMeGood) is a vendor marketplace first. They make money from vendors, not from helping couples. No tool combines task management + research + creative tools in one place. The groom market is 50% of couples and 0% served.

## P0 Features (5)

1. **Task management** — Add/edit/complete tasks, categorized (shopping, family, logistics, romance, bride). Daily view with overdue tracking.
2. **Research pages** — Curated fashion trends, shopping links, comparison boards. AI-assisted discovery.
3. **Comparison boards** — Side-by-side options for suits, venues, gifts, etc. with links and prices.
4. **Greeting card creator** — Save-the-dates, thank-yous, custom cards. AI-generated designs + templates.
5. **Shareable pages** — Every page has a clean URL shareable on WhatsApp. No login required to view.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | When |
|--------|--------|------|
| Tasks added per week | 5+ | Weekly |
| Research pages created | 3+ total | By week 2 |
| Pages shared via WhatsApp | 1+ | By week 2 |
| Forgotten tasks at wedding | 0 | July 7 |

## Platform

Web (Next.js on Vercel). Pages are URLs — shareable on WhatsApp, viewable on any device.

## Timeline

- Wedding: July 5-6, 2026 (93 days from today)
- Must be usable within 1 session of building
- Iterate weekly based on actual wedding planning needs

## Two-Year Vision

If this works for one wedding, it becomes a wedding concierge service for other couples. Gender-neutral, no vendor marketplace, purely on the couple's side.
