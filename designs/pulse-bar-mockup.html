Pulse bar = tap to mark done. No swipe. No form. No screen change.
Watermark = just visible context. Tap opens edit form.
Pill = your time commitment. Swipe to complete. Tap to edit.
Task = checkbox in bottom bar. Tap to complete.

The simple rule:

Does it take 15+ minutes of your focused time? → Pill
Is it a quick habit you just tap done? → Pulse
Is it a to-do with no time? → Task
Is it a daily ritual anchored to a time but not a block? → Watermark or Pulse (user's choice)
