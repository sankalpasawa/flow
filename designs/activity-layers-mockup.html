How different types of activities live on the same screen

The Four Layers

1. Canvas (pills) — Time blocks with duration. Your commitments.
2. Watermarks — Daily rituals at a time. Light presence on canvas.
3. Pulse bar — Micro-habits (hourly/frequent). Ambient strip, not on canvas.
4. Bottom bar — Tasks to complete. No time commitment.

Pulse bar — states and behaviors

The pulse bar is a horizontal scrollable strip between the date strip and the canvas. Each micro-habit shows as a small chip. Tap to mark done. The pulsing dot shows which one is currently due.

Hourly recurring: Shows one chip per occurrence. "Water" appears 8 times for an 8AM-10PM day.
Daily quick habits: Shows once. "Affirmations" appears once.
Done items: Strikethrough + faded. Stay visible so you see progress.
