Architecture

The Four Layers

Layer 1 Abstraction Universal truths about human-AI work
Layer 2 Operating System How any company runs: processes, rituals, sensors
Layer 3 Modules B2C, B2B, Marketplace, SaaS, Agency
Layer 4 Department Skills Design, Eng, Marketing, Sales, Ops

Each layer builds on the one above. More specific as you go down.
