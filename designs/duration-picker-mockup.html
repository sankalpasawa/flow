Three options for how the custom duration input works

Option A: Scroll Wheel RECOMMENDED

iPhone timer-style scroll picker. Hours and minutes wheels side by side. Feels native. Most familiar pattern on iOS. Tap the … chip to open, scroll to set, tap Done.

How it fits in the activity form

The picker opens inline below the duration chips. No modal, no new screen. Tap … → picker slides in with spring animation. Tap Done or pick a preset → picker slides out, chip updates to show selected value (e.g., "10m").
