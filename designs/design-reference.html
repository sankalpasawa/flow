07
Design Rules
🪟
Glass everywhere. Pills, task sheet, tab bar all use frosted glass. White at 65% + blur 20px + white border at 75%.
🚫
No accent bars. Category communicated via subtle tint (5-7% opacity) only. Never solid color fills or borders.
📝
Mindset always visible. Every pill shows icon + title + 2-line italic mindset. This is the product's soul.
🎯
One concept: Activity. Has time → pill. No time + repeat → watermark. No time + no repeat → task bar.
🧮
Simple rendering. No collision detection. Watermarks float on top. Pills never shift for watermarks.
🌡️
Warm palette only. No cold blues, no pure white surfaces, no clinical grays. Everything lives in the same warm room.
✨
Spring physics. scale(0.97) on press, cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1) for all transitions. Everything has weight.
